Weekly App Review Pulse — Week of 2026-03-09
─────────────────────────────────────────

TOP THEMES
  1. Login & OTP Issues — Users frequently report being unable to log in due to OTP delays or biometric authentication breaking after updates, causing significant frustration among daily users.
  2. Portfolio Sync Delays — Multiple users note that mutual fund and stock holdings take a long time to refresh or show incorrect balances, undermining trust in the app's accuracy.
  3. US Stocks Experience — Growing interest in the US stocks feature is tempered by complaints about order failures, currency conversion confusion, and lack of real-time price visibility.

USER VOICES
  "After the latest update my fingerprint login stopped working completely. Every time I open the app I have to re-enter my MPIN."  — Google Play, 2★
  "My mutual fund portfolio has not refreshed in 3 days. The numbers shown are completely wrong."  — Google Play, 1★
  "US stocks section is great but my order got stuck in 'processing' for 2 hours. No error, no confirmation."  — Google Play, 3★

WHAT'S WORKING
  1. Smart Save Feature: Users love the automated savings round-up and goal-based saving nudges, praising their simplicity and effectiveness.
  2. INDMoney Dashboard: The consolidated net worth view across banks, MFs, and stocks is frequently cited as the app's standout feature.
  3. Customer Support Chat: Several reviewers note quick and helpful responses from the in-app support team when issues are escalated.

ACTION IDEAS
  1. Action 1: Audit and improve OTP delivery reliability — partner with fallback SMS providers and add a resend OTP countdown to reduce login drop-offs.
  2. Action 2: Implement a portfolio refresh indicator — show users a timestamp of last sync and a manual refresh button so stale data is clearly communicated.
  3. Action 3: Add real-time US stock order status push notifications — users placing international orders need immediate confirmation or failure alerts rather than silent processing states.
